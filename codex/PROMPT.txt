=== COMMON RULES (put these in every image-model request) ===
"Redraw this sheet at 2x resolution (twice the input's width and height) as sharper, more detailed versions of

 - LAYOUT: every picture stays at exactly the same position, size and outline. Do not move, add, remove,

 - BACKGROUND: the flat pure magenta #FF00FF around the pictures stays flat pure magenta: no shadows, glow,

=== backgrounds ===
Full painted backgrounds (opaque, they fill their box completely; the magenta area beside them stays magenta).
Keep the perspective lines of walls, floors and doorways exactly: the game draws ceilings, floors, doors,
furniture and characters on top of the room-wall pictures (dunrm, plnwalls), so walls, corners and the floor
line must not move. logonscr: the painted lettering on the stone tablet must stay exactly the same words, in
the same serif letters, size and place - repaint the letters crisply, never change or invent text. titlescr:
keep the banner lettering "Mind Maze" exactly.

=== layers ===
Ceilings and floor coverings that the game lays over the room walls. They are drawn in perspective and must
keep their exact outline, angle and width (635-636 px) so they still line up with the walls. Rugs: keep the
pattern, only crisper. Ceilings: wooden beams and rafters with visible wood grain.

=== doors ===
Doors placed into the room walls: keep each outline exactly so it fits its wall opening. Wooden plank doors
with iron or brass fittings in stone arches; the tower doorway shows a stone spiral staircase. The small
square sign with the red down arrow stays a simple sign.

=== characters ===
People of a medieval fairy-tale court painted in the same storybook style: the duke with his parrot, the
jester, the king, the queen with her train bearers, the sorceress, the alchemist, travellers, ladies,
servants, a man in the stocks, suits of armour. Keep every face (age, expression, direction of the eyes),
costume, colour and pose exactly; more detail in faces, hands, fabric and props. Some characters stand on a
piece of floor or furniture (jack-in-the-box, rug, column) - keep those parts too. Do not make anyone more
cartoonish or more realistic than the input.

=== furniture ===
Furniture and decorations for the rooms (tables, chairs, bookshelf, chest, vases, candles, banners, weapons).
Keep each outline and perspective exactly; more detail in wood grain, fabric, metal and glaze.

=== pictures ===
Small framed pictures that hang on the walls: each shows a black-and-white engraving-style icon for one area of
interest (atom, skeleton, globe, hourglass, people, symbols, ...) - keep the icon and frame exactly, only
sharper. Also the award pictures (ribbon rosette, medal, ring, trophy, empty plaque) on their beige squares.

=== overlays ===
Parchment panels the game writes text onto: an illuminated letter Q banner and a plain speech parchment.
Keep the writing areas EMPTY parchment - no text, lines or new decoration. Sharpen the illuminated Q, the
hooks and the zig-zag cut edges.

=== ui ===
Control-panel buttons on their beige background. Each picture is a strip of 4 states of the SAME button side
by side (normal, highlighted, faded/disabled, pressed): keep the 4 copies in their exact places and sizes, the
same symbol in each, only the colours differ as in the input. Calligraphic numerals 1-4 stay the same numerals.

=== pages ===
Story pages made of printed text. Copy every letter exactly (same words, line breaks, font and size). If the
model cannot keep the text exactly readable, keep the page as it is and only sharpen it. Never rewrite text.
