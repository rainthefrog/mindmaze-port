MindMaze

MindMaze uses the pictures, sounds and questions from your own copy of Microsoft Encarta: 2006 Premium, or Encarta 95 (MPEG edition), 96 (Macintosh), 97 or 98 (US English). They are copied once into this app; the disc is not needed afterwards.

Made on a PC: in the Windows game, Settings > "Export data for the phone app". Quickest.

Or from the disc:

The five files are ENCARTA.EXE, ENCARTAR.DLL, MINDMAZE.DB, MINDMAZE.IDX and MINDMAZE.EIT (folder EE\ENCARTA on the Encarta 2006 disc). For Encarta 95 to 98 choose the disc image.
