Task: WIDESCREEN extensions for MindMaze pictures with your IMAGE GENERATION / image editing model (outpainting),
one sheet at a time.

Input folder:  <sheets-folder>   (default: art/work/wide/sheets in MindMaze's data folder; 20 sheets, all
                                  768x512, with masks; made by MindMaze on its first start)
Output folder: <output-folder>   (default: art/work/wide/out_gen in the data folder)
BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE: if <sheets-folder> or <output-folder> above still reads literally like that (not
replaced by a real path), ask the user where the sheets are and where the results should go, offering the
defaults, and wait for the answer. Check that the input folder holds the *.png sheets with a .txt next to each;
if not, tell the user to start MindMaze once (it writes them) and stop.
(Below, "wide\sheets\" means <sheets-folder> and "out_gen\" / "wide\out_gen\" mean <output-folder>.)

Write each result to <output-folder>\<same file name>.png - exactly 2x the sheet: 1536x1024.
Skip sheets that already exist in <output-folder>.
Every sheet has a .txt next to it (same name) with:
  ACTION    FILL IN DETAILS or STRETCH (see below)
  PICTURE   the picture's box and what it shows
  PAINT     the flat pure green #00FF00 area(s) to paint
  TASK      what the green area must show for this picture
  GEOMETRY  exact lines and points the painting must follow (sheet pixels, the 2x result's pixels in brackets)
Read it before generating and put ALL of it (ACTION, description, PAINT, TASK, Extra and every GEOMETRY line)
into the request, together with the COMMON RULES, the ALIGNMENT RULES and the section for the ACTION.
The masks in codex\wide\masks\<name>.png (white = green paint area) show the paint area exactly; use them as
the edit mask if your image tool takes one.

ORDER: 1) backgrounds  2) layers  3) objects, each group in name order.

Request 2x the sheet size from the image model (1536x1024). Never produce a result with a script or program
(no PIL, ImageMagick, OpenCV, ESRGAN, resizing, mirroring, copying, cloning or stretching pixels into the green
area): every sheet must go through the image model. Do not crop, pad, add margins or rearrange anything. Never
give the model an earlier RESULT (from wide\out_gen\, codex\out_gen\ or codex\hd\) as an input image: only the
sheet from wide\sheets\ (and its mask).

=== COMMON RULES (put these in every image-model request) ===
"Redraw this sheet at 2x resolution (twice the input's width and height). MindMaze (Microsoft Encarta, 1990s):
 hand-painted fairy-tale castle illustrations in a classic storybook style - gouache / acrylic painting with
 soft brush texture, warm light, rich but natural colours, clean painted edges.
 - Everything that is not green stays exactly as it is: same position, size, outline, content and colours,
   only redrawn sharper.
 - The flat GREEN area(s) are empty canvas: paint them as described. Fill them completely: no green may be left
   in the result, and nothing is painted outside the picture's box.
 - Do not mirror or copy parts of the picture into the green area; continue it logically.
 - Nothing new and eye-catching in the green area: no new people, animals, doors, windows, furniture, symbols,
   lettering or light sources unless the TASK says so.
 - COLOURS: keep the original hue, saturation, brightness and colour temperature. No new colour casts. The
   painted area is exactly as bright, as saturated and as detailed as the picture right next to it.
 - MAGENTA: the flat pure magenta #FF00FF area stays flat pure magenta: no shadows, glow, gradients, texture
   or outlines on it, and no magenta inside the painting. Edges between painting and magenta are clean and
   sharp (no halo).
 - No borders, frames, labels, captions, signatures or watermarks."

=== ALIGNMENT RULES (put these in every image-model request; they keep the pieces lining up in the game) ===
"The game puts these pictures on top of each other (walls, then ceiling, floor covering, doors, furniture,
 characters), and the walls and every layer are extended separately. They only line up if every extension
 follows the same strict geometry:
 1. SEAM: at the border between the picture and the green area every edge, line, stone course, beam, plank,
    pattern row and colour continues with no offset, no step, no bend and no gap. Nothing ends at the border.
 2. STRAIGHT LINES: every line listed under GEOMETRY is a straight line from its border point to its end point.
    Follow it exactly (within 2 px at 2x). Do not curve it, do not change its angle.
 3. PERSPECTIVE: lines that run into the depth of the room meet at the GEOMETRY vanishing point. Lines parallel
    to the back wall stay exactly horizontal. Upright edges (posts, wall corners, door sides, table legs) stay
    exactly vertical. No lens curve, no fisheye, no tilt.
 4. SCALE: things in the green area have the same size as next to the border (stone blocks, planks, rug
    pattern, checkerboard squares, beams), only growing slightly where perspective brings them closer.
 5. OUTLINE: a layer or object ends exactly where its GEOMETRY says; beyond that it is flat magenta. Do not
    widen, round off or shorten an outline at the seam.
 6. STATIC SCENE: do not move, resize or redraw anything outside the green area to make the extension fit."

=== ACTION: FILL IN DETAILS (all sheets except backgrounds_000) ===
The picture keeps its size and place in the game: the view simply becomes wider, like a camera that sees
further to the side. Paint what lies beyond the old edge, following the TASK and the GEOMETRY.

=== ACTION: STRETCH (backgrounds_000 = the sign-in screen) ===
This picture has its own ornamental border frame. On the sheet the frame bars and corner rosettes have
already been moved out to the new, wider edges, and the green gaps lie between the moved frame and the rest of
the picture. Close the gaps so the picture looks as if it had always been this wide: the top and bottom frame
bands continue straight through the gaps (same height, thickness and rope ornament), the corner rosettes stay
round and complete, and inside the frame the castle wall and paving continue. The stone tablet with its
lettering keeps its size and its letters exactly; no new letters anywhere.

=== backgrounds ===
Full opaque scene paintings with green on both sides: the green becomes opaque painting (no magenta). In the
rooms the side walls run towards the viewer; in the green strips they continue outwards along the GEOMETRY
floor line, and their stone courses / panels follow the vanishing point.

=== layers ===
Ceilings (wooden beams, rafters, posts) and floor coverings (rugs, checkerboard floor) drawn in perspective on
magenta. Continue the layer only as far as its GEOMETRY lines allow: where a GEOMETRY line says the edge leaves
the picture, the layer ends there and everything beyond is flat magenta. The checkerboard rows stay
horizontal and the squares keep their size.

=== objects ===
A character or object cut off at the left or right edge of the room view. Paint the part that was outside the
frame into the green strip, starting exactly where GEOMETRY says the outline crosses the border, with the same
width and direction, matching the visible part (same person, clothes, colours, light). Where nothing of it
would be, flat magenta. Do not change the visible part; do not add another person or object.

=== CHECK each result before moving on (measuring with a script is allowed; changing pixels is not) ===
- No green pixels left (no pixel with G > 200 and R, B < 80).
- At every GEOMETRY point (2x coordinates) the line or edge is within 4 px of the given position.
- The seam: compare a 6 px wide column on each side of each border; no visible jump in colour or brightness.
If a sheet fails a check, generate it ONE more time with the same request plus a sentence naming what was
wrong (for example "the floor line must end at (4, 572)"). Keep whichever of the two results passes more checks;
never more than 2 generations per sheet. Write a line per sheet to out_gen\CHECK.txt: name, which checks passed,
and whether it was generated twice.
